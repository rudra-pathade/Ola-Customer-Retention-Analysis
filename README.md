# 🚖 Ola Customer Retention Analysis

A complete end-to-end **Data Analytics & Business Intelligence** project focused on understanding **customer retention, ride performance, booking behavior, cancellations, payment preferences, and customer satisfaction** using **Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI**.

This project transforms raw ride-booking data into actionable business insights through **data cleaning, SQL analysis, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and an interactive Power BI dashboard** to help improve customer retention and operational efficiency.

# 📌 Overview

Customer retention is one of the most critical success factors in the ride-hailing industry. Frequent cancellations, long waiting times, inconsistent service quality, and poor customer experience can significantly impact customer loyalty.

This project analyzes **150,000 ride booking records** to identify customer behavior patterns, booking trends, cancellation reasons, payment preferences, and operational bottlenecks. The insights generated can help businesses improve customer satisfaction, reduce churn, and optimize overall ride operations.

# 🔄 Project Workflow

### 1️⃣ Data Collection
- Imported the raw ride booking dataset.
- Performed initial inspection and validation.

### 2️⃣ Data Cleaning
- Removed duplicate records.
- Handled missing values.
- Standardized booking status and categorical values.
- Converted date and time columns.
- Validated ratings and payment methods.

### 3️⃣ Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Analyzed booking trends.
- Examined customer and driver ratings.
- Explored ride distance and booking values.
- Identified customer behavior patterns.
- Studied cancellation trends.

### 4️⃣ SQL Analysis
- Imported the cleaned dataset into PostgreSQL.
- Wrote business-focused SQL queries.
- Calculated KPIs and operational metrics.
- Performed customer segmentation and aggregations.

### 5️⃣ Power BI Dashboard
- Designed an interactive dashboard.
- Built KPI cards using DAX.
- Added filters and slicers.
- Visualized booking performance and customer insights.

### 6️⃣ Business Reporting
- Prepared a professional business report.
- Created a presentation summarizing key findings and recommendations.

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

- More than **93,000 rides** were successfully completed.
- Total revenue exceeded **₹51.8 Million**.
- Average booking value was approximately **₹508**.
- Customers maintained an average rating of **4.40/5**, indicating high satisfaction.
- **UPI** emerged as the most preferred payment method.
- Driver cancellations were one of the major reasons for unsuccessful bookings.
- Operational efficiency had a direct impact on customer retention.
- Faster ride fulfillment significantly improved customer satisfaction and repeat usage.
